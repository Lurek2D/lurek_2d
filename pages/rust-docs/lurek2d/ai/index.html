Exposes the AI subsystem module. Groups the full AI runtime surface into one coherent module boundary for decision and control. Exposes complementary layers for actor state, sensing, planning, steering, coordination, and tooling. Keeps integration predictable by publishing shared types through a single composition entry point. Aligns tactical and strategic subsystems under consistent data flow and update expectations. Defines the high-level contract of engine-side intelligence capabilities available to the rest of runtime.
