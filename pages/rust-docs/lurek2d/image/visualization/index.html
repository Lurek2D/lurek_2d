lurek2d::image

Module visualization

Image visualizations for debugging and analysis. High-level visualization module wiring that groups image-debug renderers by domain. Re-exports category entry points to provide one flat surface for visualization consumers. Shares internal facade utilities while keeping submodule responsibilities clearly separated.
